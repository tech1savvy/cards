# How does Transit Gateway integrate with Direct Connect?

---

Transit Gateway can connect to a **Direct Connect Gateway**.

Result:

On-prem data center  
 ↓  
Direct Connect  
 ↓  
Transit Gateway  
 ↓  
Many VPCs across accounts and regions

Benefit:

- one physical connection can serve **many VPCs**.

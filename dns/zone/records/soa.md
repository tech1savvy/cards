# What is a DNS SOA (Start of Authority) record?

---

A mandatory DNS record that stores administrative info about a zone:
- **MNAME** — primary nameserver for the zone
- **RNAME** — admin email (`.` replaces `@`, e.g. `admin.example.com` = `admin@example.com`)

---

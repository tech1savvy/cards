# What are the three levels of Sentinel enforcement?

---

- **Advisory** — violation is logged but the plan is allowed to pass. An advisory message is shown.
- **Soft Mandatory** — policy must pass unless an administrator provides an override. Overrides are admin-only.
- **Hard Mandatory** — policy must pass regardless. Cannot be overridden unless the policy itself is removed. This is Sentinel's default enforcement level.
